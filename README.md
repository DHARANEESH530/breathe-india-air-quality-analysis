# Breathe India – Air Quality Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview

Breathe India is an air quality analysis project focused on understanding pollution levels across Indian States and Union Territories.

The project analyses current AQI levels, maximum AQI records, highly polluted districts, and major pollution sources using Excel and Power BI.

An interactive dashboard was developed to compare regional air quality conditions, explore pollution patterns, and identify areas experiencing higher pollution levels.

## 2. Problem Statement

Air pollution is a major environmental concern that affects cities, districts, and communities.

Understanding regional AQI variations and identifying major pollution sources are important for evaluating air quality conditions.

This project aims to compare AQI levels across Indian States and Union Territories, identify highly polluted districts, examine maximum AQI records, and explore the contribution of different pollution sources.

## 3. Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection, preparation, and organisation.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and preparation within Power BI.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and version control.

- **Coverage:** 35 Indian States and Union Territories
- **Primary Metric:** Air Quality Index (AQI)
- **Data Format:** Excel
- **Dashboard Tool:** Power BI

### Dataset Source

## 5. Data Preprocessing

Excel was used to organise and prepare the air quality information for dashboard analysis.

The following activities were performed:

- Examined the structure and columns of the datasets.
- Organised air quality information across different Indian regions.
- Prepared state and Union Territory-level AQI information.
- Organised district-level AQI records for regional comparisons.
- Prepared pollution-source information for source-wise analysis.
- Structured the data for integration and visualization in Power BI.

## 6. Analysis Performed

### A. Overall Air Quality Analysis
- Analysed average current AQI across 35 Indian States and Union Territories.
- Examined maximum AQI values.
- Compared current AQI conditions across regions.

### B. State & Union Territory Comparison
- Compared current AQI levels across different regions.
- Identified regions with comparatively higher AQI values.
- Examined differences in maximum AQI records.

### C. District-Level Pollution Analysis
- Identified the top 10 most polluted districts based on average AQI.
- Compared AQI levels across highly polluted districts.
- Explored regional pollution patterns.

### D. Pollution Source Analysis
Examined the contribution of major pollution sources, including:

- Construction
- Industrial Activities
- Vehicles
- Crop Burning
- Waste Burning

### E. Key Influencers Analysis
- Used Power BI Key Influencers to explore factors associated with AQI variations.
- Investigated relationships between pollution-source information and district-level AQI.
